Beauty salon FAQ, Singapore

How do I book an appointment in Singapore?

Message us on WhatsApp at +65 9870 6393 or use the booking form below. Tell us the service you want and your preferred date, and we will confirm availability.

What beauty services do you offer?

Hair cuts, facials, hair treatments such as keratin and hair spa, bleach, eyebrow and face threading, face and body waxing, body scrub, manicure and pedicure, bridal makeup, saree tying, hair do's and a full range of mehandi (henna) designs.

Do you do bridal makeup and bridal mehandi?

Yes. We offer bridal makeup, bridal and engagement mehandi, saree tying and hair styling. Please book early and share your wedding date and outfit on WhatsApp.

How can I find out the price of a service?

Send us a WhatsApp message with the service you are interested in and we will reply with the current price.

Do you offer services for men?

Yes. Our menu includes men's eyebrow threading, and you can message us on WhatsApp to ask about other services.
